About me

My love for media started with YouTube. What began as simply watching videos and discovering creators gradually grew into an appreciation for the creativity, storytelling, and connection behind the content I enjoyed. Over time, that interest turned into a passion of my own, inspiring me to experiment with filming, editing, and creating videos that capture moments, tell stories, and connect with others.

Today, I've been able to turn that personal interest into professional experience. Through my consulting club at UC Berkeley, I'm working with GEEM, a small business, to strengthen its social media presence. From brainstorming video concepts tailored to its target audience of Millennial women to filming and editing content, I've gained firsthand experience in the strategy and creativity behind producing engaging digital media.

Whether I'm creating videos for myself or developing content for a business, I love the process of bringing an idea to life and finding ways to connect with an audience. I'm especially fascinated by how media and entertainment shape the way we see the world, share experiences, and build communities. This portfolio is a collection of that journey, bringing together my personal creativity and professional experiences.
